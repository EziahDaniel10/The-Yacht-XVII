The Yacht XVII – Vessel Rules & Guest Conduct

To ensure the safety, comfort, and enjoyment of all guests, the following rules are strictly enforced aboard The Yacht XVII.

Safety & Legal Compliance
	•	No weapons of any kind are permitted onboard.
	•	No illegal drugs are allowed. Any violation will result in immediate termination of the charter, and the U.S. Coast Guard will be notified.
	•	No marijuana onboard the vessel.
Although marijuana is legal in some states, including the District of Columbia, it remains illegal on federal waterways. Smoking marijuana onboard is prohibited.
In select cases, guests may advise the crew in advance, and a designated smoking stop may be arranged at the captain’s discretion.

⸻

Smoking Policy
	•	No smoking on the vessel interior.
	•	We do offer a designated cigar area for guest enjoyment, as approved by the crew.

⸻

Conduct & Guest Experience
	•	No standing on furniture at any time.
	•	No arguing or disruptive behavior — good vibes only.
	•	All guests must follow the instructions and rules of the captain and crew at all times — no exceptions.

⸻

Restroom (Head) Etiquette

Yacht restrooms are not the same as land-based restrooms.
	•	Do not flush tampons, wipes, food, paper towels, or any non-approved items.
	•	Only the dissolvable toilet paper provided onboard may be flushed.
	•	Failure to follow restroom guidelines may result in immediate system stoppage, and a damage/cleaning fee will be applied.

⸻

Docking & Boarding
	•	Guests must comply with all dock rules and marina regulations when boarding and disembarking the vessel.
	•	Early boarding is not permitted. Charter time begins at the scheduled departure.

⸻

Decorations
	•	Decorations are permitted by prior arrangement only.
	•	Guests must coordinate with the crew at the time of booking.
	•	A $50 decoration fee applies and includes up to 30 minutes of setup time.
	•	Decorations must not damage the vessel, leave residue, obstruct walkways, or interfere with the captain’s visibility
